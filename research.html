研究项目

高温超导射频 SQUID 磁强计微纳加工与移动测试

2017.10 — 2020.07

北大硕士期间

研究目标

开发液氮温区下工作的高温超导（HTS）射频 SQUID，将其应用边界从静态的实验室屏蔽室拓展至开放、无屏蔽的真实物理环境。

核心贡献

  • 微纳加工与屏蔽调优：熟练掌握 HTS 薄膜的光刻与器件微纳加工工艺；设计并构建了屏蔽补偿系统，大幅提升了基础磁屏蔽室内的器件工作稳定性。
  • 移动测试平台搭建：设计并搭建了可在无屏蔽户外环境中锁定并移动的高温超导 SQUID 移动测量平台，成功采集并分析了在不同噪声背景及姿态变化下的器件运行特征。

基于 MgB2 薄膜的集总参数动态电感探测器（LEKID）

2018.10 — 2019.10

北大硕士期间

研究目标

探索二硼化镁（MgB2）薄膜在动态电感探测器中的应用潜力，服务于先进的 X 射线与光学辐射探测。

核心贡献

  • 微波测量与性能评估：通过微波测试，观测 MgB2 LEKID 的光学响应与准粒子寿命，对其在高灵敏度 X 射线探测领域的应用前景做了技术可行性评估。

二维纳米材料的第一性原理计算

2014.10 — 2016.11

武汉理工本科期间

研究目标

采用密度泛函理论（DFT）计算方法，深入研究二维纳米材料的电子、磁学及光学性质。

核心贡献

  • 缺陷与掺杂分析：研究锰（Mn）原子钝化交错硅烯纳米条带（ZSiNRs）的化学掺杂效应，借助自旋极化理论阐明其半金属性质、自旋过滤行为及电子输运机制。
  • 光学性质建模：利用第一性原理赝势方法，分析单层和双层金（Au）掺杂磷烯的电子结构与光学特性，评估其在光电子器件领域的应用潜力。

Research projects

HTS rf SQUID magnetometer fabrication and mobile testing

Oct 2017 — July 2020

M.Sc. at Peking University

Research scope

Developed high-temperature superconducting (HTS) rf SQUIDs operating at liquid-nitrogen temperature, moving them from static laboratory shielding rooms toward mobile, unshielded environments.

Core contributions

  • Microfabrication and shielding: Worked through photolithography and fabrication for HTS films; engineered a shielding-compensation system to improve stability inside a basic magnetic shielding room.
  • Mobile testing platform: Built an HTS SQUID mobile platform that can lock and move in unshielded conditions, and measured operational characteristics under different noise backgrounds and attitude changes.

Lumped-element kinetic inductance detectors based on MgB2 thin films

Oct 2018 — Oct 2019

M.Sc. at Peking University

Research scope

Explored magnesium diboride (MgB2) thin films in kinetic inductance detectors for X-ray and optical radiation detection.

Core contributions

  • Microwave measurement: Microwave tests of the optical response and quasiparticle lifetime of MgB2 LEKIDs, to assess feasibility for high-sensitivity X-ray detection.

First-principles computation of 2D nanomaterials

Oct 2014 — Nov 2016

B.Sc. at Wuhan University of Technology

Research scope

Investigated electronic, magnetic, and optical properties of two-dimensional nanomaterials using density-functional theory (DFT).

Core contributions

  • Defect and doping analysis: Chemical doping of zigzag silicene nanoribbons (ZSiNRs) passivated with Mn, including half-metallicity, spin filtering, and transport via spin-polarization theory.
  • Optical property modeling: Electronic and optical characteristics of single- and double-Au-doped phosphorene with first-principles pseudopotential methods, for possible optoelectronic use.
