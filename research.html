2017.10 — 2020.07 · Peking University

HTS rf SQUID magnetometer fabrication and mobile tests

HTS SQUID Magnetometer

  1. Overcame fabrication and shielding-compensation issues to develop HTS rf SQUID samples with consistent performance.
  2. Established and tested an environmental magnetic-field compensation system using HTS SQUID magnetometers as background-field detectors, which improved the working environment in the simple magnetic shielding room at Peking University.
  3. Developed an HTS SQUID mobile platform that can lock and move in an unshielded environment. Operational characteristics were measured under different noise backgrounds and attitude changes.

2014.10 — 2016.11 · Wuhan University of Technology

Magnetic and transport properties of two-dimensional nanomaterials, first-principles computation

DFT Silicene Phosphorene

  1. Investigated chemical doping of zigzag silicene nanoribbons (ZSiNRs), with Mn replacing H at one edge, by first-principles calculations.
  2. Discussed the half-metallic properties of doped ZSiNRs using spin-polarization theory.
  3. Explained the spin-filtering property of doped ZSiNRs from the transmission spectrum and molecular-orbital theory, showing a possible path to spintronic devices.
